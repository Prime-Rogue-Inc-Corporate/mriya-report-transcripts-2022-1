# Copyright, fair dealing and the limits of what is licensed here

A transcript is a derivative work. That single fact governs everything below.

## The layered position

| Layer | Whose | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| The spoken words | The speakers and the broadcast's rights holders | **Not licensed here.** Reproduced under fair dealing |
| The transcription, labelling, timestamps | Prime Rogue Inc. | CC BY 4.0 |
| Structure, metadata, checksums, manifests | Prime Rogue Inc. | CC BY 4.0 |
| Editorial notes and corrections | Prime Rogue Inc. | CC BY 4.0 |

Prime Rogue Inc. **does not assert and does not purport to grant** any right in the
underlying spoken content. A reader who treats the CC BY grant as covering the speech has
misread it, and this document exists so that misreading is not available.

## The fair dealing basis

Copyright Act, ss. 29–29.2: research, private study, criticism, review and **news
reporting**. This repository exists to support published journalism about the Mriya Aid and
Mriya Report organisations, and is relied on for that purpose.

Fair dealing for criticism, review and news reporting is **conditional on attribution** —
the source, and where given in the source, the author, performer, maker or broadcaster.
Every transcript here carries that attribution. **A transcript that loses its attribution
loses the dealing's basis**, which is why attribution is a structural requirement of this
repository and not a courtesy.

Fair dealing is assessed on the dealing as a whole, including its purpose, character,
amount, alternatives, the nature of the work and the effect on the market for it. Posting
transcripts of a free public information broadcast, for journalism, in a form that does not
substitute for the recording, is a conventional dealing. **That is a position, not a
guarantee, and it is not legal advice.**

## Minimisation — who appears and who does not

Live broadcasts include people who are not the subject of anything.

- **Principals and public figures, speaking publicly in their public capacity:** named and
  transcribed.
- **Incidental participants, callers and ordinary members of the public:** **not named.**
  Transcribed as `[participant]` where their contribution is needed for sense, and omitted
  where it is not.
- **Personal information disclosed in passing** — addresses, contact details, family,
  health, anything about a named person's private life — is **not transcribed**, and the
  omission is marked.

A public transcript repository is the most exposed artifact in any investigation. These
rules are what make it defensible.

## Takedown and correction

Rights holders, and anyone named, may write to **kevin@primerogueinc.com**. Substantive
accuracy corrections are made against the recording and recorded in the correction log,
not silently edited. A rights-holder complaint is answered on the merits.
