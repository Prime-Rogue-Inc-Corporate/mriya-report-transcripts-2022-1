# Confirmation clips

Short padded audio clips cut at flagged timestamps, for the publisher to confirm by ear.
Generated by `tools/make_clips.py`; the checklist is `CONFIRM.md`.

A clip is **never** the artifact of record. The artifact of record is the full source file,
hashed in `MANIFEST.csv` and `SHA256SUMS`. A clip exists only so a human can answer one
question: are those the words, and is that the speaker?

Clips of unconfirmed passages are not published, quoted or sent to third parties.
